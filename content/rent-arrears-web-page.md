# Your rent account is in arrears

Your rent account is in arrears. You owe **£318.60**, and you need to pay it so that it
reaches us on or before **1 August 2026**. Arrears means rent you owe from earlier weeks
that has not yet been paid, on top of the rent for the current week.

## How to pay

There are 3 ways to pay what you owe:

1. Pay online. Go to [www.banchester.gov.uk/pay-rent](https://www.banchester.gov.uk/pay-rent)
   and quote your rent account number, 4471-0932. This is the quickest way to pay.
2. Pay by telephone using a debit or credit card. Call 01000 000 000 and have your rent
   account number ready.
3. Set up a Direct Debit so that the rent is paid automatically each month. A Direct
   Debit is an instruction to your bank to pay us on a set date. Call 01000 000 000 to
   arrange one.

## If you cannot pay in full

If you cannot pay the full £318.60 by 1 August 2026, contact the Housing Rents Team
before that date on 01000 000 000. Do not ignore this. If you tell us you are struggling
to pay, we may be able to agree a payment arrangement with you.

## If the amount does not look right

If the amount does not look right to you, contact the Housing Rents Team on 01000 000 000
and explain what you think is wrong. We will check your account and correct it if there
has been a mistake. Continue to pay any amount you agree is correct while the query is
being looked into. If you have already paid the £318.60 in full, thank you, and you do
not need to do anything.
